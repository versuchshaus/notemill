How to use Notemill

  1. Open an article and press ⇧⌘U, or click the Notemill icon in the toolbar.
  2. Only want part of the page? Select it first: Notemill then keeps only your selection.
  3. In the reading view, choose Send to Notion, Save .md, Copy Markdown or Print.

Keys in the reading view

Enter
Send to Notion is ready as soon as the reading view opens: Enter opens it, Enter again saves.
Tab Shift+Tab
Move along the buttons of the bar. Enter or Space presses the one you are on.
↑ ↓
Pick a suggested tag while typing tags.
Space Shift+Space
Scroll down or up by a screen, also right after opening: only Enter sends to Notion.
Esc
Close the open row. Press it again to leave the reading view.

Opened with a click and the keys do nothing? Some browsers keep the keyboard in their toolbar after a click. Open Notemill with your shortcut instead: it leaves the keyboard on the page.
